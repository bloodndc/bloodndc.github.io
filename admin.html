Moderator access

This dashboard opens only for UIDs listed in ADMIN_UIDS (assets/js/config.js).

Your session UID (identifies this browser — created automatically, no account needed):
—
  1. Press Copy UID above.
  2. In the repository, open assets/js/config.js and paste it inside the ADMIN_UIDS array — e.g. ADMIN_UIDS = ['PASTE-HERE'].
  3. Commit & redeploy, then press Reload below.
What is a UID and where do I get one?
When anyone opens this site, Firebase Authentication signs them in anonymously (no email, no password) and assigns a random UID. That UID is what you see in the box above — it appears automatically once the site can reach Firebase.

If the box shows a dash, two things must be done once in the Firebase console: (1) Authentication → Sign-in method → Anonymous → Enable, and (2) publish firestore.rules under Firestore → Rules. Then reload this page and your UID will appear.
