How it works

Three steps between a request and a donor

1

Post the need

A family member or hospital volunteer posts the patient’s blood group, hospital and deadline. It appears on the live board instantly.

2

Donors get alerted

Everyone who turned on emergency alerts is notified, and matching donors can be sorted by distance from the hospital.

3

Confirm and donate

The requester reveals the donor’s number, confirms the requirement with the blood bank, and the donor walks in with an ID.

Privacy by default

Your number is never on public display

Donor phone numbers stay masked. A requester has to tap “Show contact”, and every reveal is rate-limited per browser to stop scraping and spam. Read the full privacy policy to see exactly what is stored.
