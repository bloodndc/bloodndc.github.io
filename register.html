Before you register

  • You must weigh at least 50 kg and be 18–65 years old.
  • Wait 56 days between whole-blood donations.
  • Skip donation if you have a fever, are on antibiotics, or donated blood in the last 8 weeks.
  • Eat normally and drink plenty of water beforehand.
  • Bring your national ID or birth certificate to the blood bank.

Why it matters: Bangladesh needs roughly 2.4 million units of blood a year, and voluntary donors cover only a part of that.
